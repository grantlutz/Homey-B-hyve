Control your Orbit B-hyve smart watering products from Homey. Hose faucet timers, multi-zone sprinkler controllers, and flood sensors all become Homey devices: start or stop watering any zone for exactly as long as you want, pause everything with a rain delay when the weather turns, run and adjust your B-hyve schedules from Flows, and track water usage per zone in Insights. Thanks to B-hyve's live connection, watering started from a schedule or the B-hyve phone app shows up in Homey within seconds — and flood sensors raise standard Homey alarms the moment a leak or freeze is detected.

This is an unofficial community app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Orbit. A B-hyve account is required, and hose faucet timers need the B-hyve Wi-Fi hub.
