Control your Orbit B-hyve smart watering products from Homey — start and stop watering, react to rain, and keep an eye on every zone, right alongside the rest of your smart home.

This is an unofficial community app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Orbit Irrigation Products. It connects to the same B-hyve cloud your B-hyve phone app uses, so everything stays in sync in both places.

SUPPORTED HARDWARE

- B-hyve hose faucet timers (connected through the B-hyve Wi-Fi hub)
- B-hyve indoor/outdoor multi-zone sprinkler controllers
- B-hyve flood and temperature sensors

A B-hyve account and cloud connectivity are required. Bluetooth-only timers that have never been paired to a Wi-Fi hub cannot be controlled.

WATERING

Every watering zone becomes its own device. Tap to water with your default runtime, or use the "Water for" slider to start a manual run of any length — no Flow needed. While watering you see the time remaining count down, and every run adds to the zone's water usage meter, so Insights shows exactly how much water your garden gets.

STAY AHEAD OF THE WEATHER

Rain coming? Flip the rain delay toggle or let a Flow do it for you: pause all watering for any number of hours based on your favorite weather app, and get notified when a delay starts or ends. Zones with Smart Watering show their soil moisture, and you can even tell Orbit how wet the soil really is so it replans.

FLOWS FOR EVERYTHING

Trigger on watering started or finished (with zone, program, minutes, and gallons as tags), rain delays, mode changes, and station faults. Run, enable, or disable your B-hyve programs by name, or set a program's watering budget for easy seasonal adjustment. Flood sensors work with Homey's standard water and temperature alarm cards — shut the water schedule down automatically the moment a leak is detected.

ALWAYS UP TO DATE

The app listens to B-hyve's live push connection, so watering started from the B-hyve phone app, a schedule, or the timer itself shows up in Homey within seconds. Battery levels, faults, and connectivity are monitored, and if your B-hyve login ever expires a simple repair signs you back in.
